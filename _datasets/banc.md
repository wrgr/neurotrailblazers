Every fly connectome before BANC stopped at a boundary. The brain datasets cut descending
neurons off where they left the head, and the nerve-cord datasets cut the same neurons off
where they arrived. BANC reconstructs both in one volume, so a descending neuron can be
followed from the brain region that drives it to the motor circuit it reaches.

**What it is good for.** Anything that crosses the neck. The paper's main finding depends on
it. Effector neurons are driven mostly by sensory neurons in the same body part, forming local
feedback loops. Long-range ascending and descending circuits, organized into what the authors
call behavior-centric modules, link those loops, and brain regions involved in learning and
navigation supervise them. No single-region dataset could have supported that claim.

**What it does not support.** Numbers this catalog can cite. See the source note below.
