Bock and colleagues imaged orientation tuning in mouse primary visual cortex by
two-photon calcium imaging, then reconstructed the same tissue by serial-section TEM — about
1,500 cell bodies in roughly 12 TB. It established the structure-and-function paradigm that
MICrONS later scaled up.

**What it is good for.** Seeing the origin of the co-registration problem, which is still the
hard part of every functional connectomics experiment.

**What it does not support.** Dense connectivity analysis. Only a fraction of the volume was
reconstructed, and by modern standards the segmentation is sparse and manual.
