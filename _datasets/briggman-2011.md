This is the cleanest early demonstration that a wiring asymmetry explains a computation.
Direction-selective ganglion cells were identified functionally, then the retina was imaged by
serial block-face SEM and the starburst amacrine inputs traced. The wiring was asymmetric in
exactly the way the direction preference predicted.

**What it is good for.** The canonical worked example of a structural claim earning its
functional interpretation — the standard the site's rubrics point at.

**What it does not support.** Generalisation beyond this circuit. The retina is unusually
tractable: a thin, layered, well-characterised structure with a known computation to explain.
