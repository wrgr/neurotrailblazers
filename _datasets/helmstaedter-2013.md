Helmstaedter and colleagues densely reconstructed 950 neurons of the mouse inner plexiform
layer, the retina's main computational neuropil, from serial block-face EM. They combined
crowd-sourced manual annotation with machine-learning segmentation. The paper is as much
about how the annotation was organized as about the circuit, which makes it an origin point
for the modern proofreading-as-labor problem. It also described a new bipolar cell type.

**What it is good for.** The historical argument for automation. Read it alongside the
segmentation unit to see what the field was doing before flood-filling networks.

**What it does not support.** Direct comparison of its error rates with modern pipelines. The
annotation protocol and the quality metrics are not the ones in use today.
