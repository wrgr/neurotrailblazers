Helmstaedter and colleagues densely reconstructed a volume of mouse inner plexiform
layer using serial block-face EM, with a large team of trained annotators. It is the origin of
the modern proofreading-as-labour problem: the paper is as much about how the annotation was
organised as about the circuit.

**What it is good for.** The historical argument for automation. Read it alongside the
segmentation unit to see what the field was doing before flood-filling networks.

**What it does not support.** Direct comparison of its error rates to modern pipelines; the
annotation protocol and the quality metrics are not the ones in use today.
