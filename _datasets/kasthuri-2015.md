"Saturated" means nothing was skipped — every process in roughly 1,700 μm³ of mouse
neocortex was segmented, glia and vasculature included. The volume is tiny by modern standards
and the completeness is the point.

**What it is good for.** Understanding what dense reconstruction actually demands, and what the
neuropil looks like when nothing is filtered out. It is also the dataset behind the widely cited
finding that axon-dendrite proximity is a poor predictor of connection.

**What it does not support.** Any statistical claim that needs many cells. At this volume most
neurons are truncated within a few tens of microns of their soma.
