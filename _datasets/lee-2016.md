Lee and colleagues combined in vivo calcium imaging with EM reconstruction to test whether
layer 2/3 pyramidal neurons with similar orientation preference are preferentially connected.
They are. Axons and dendrites of all preferences passed within 5 μm of each other at roughly
equal rates, so the specificity is set at the scale of spines, not by which arbors happen to
overlap. MICrONS later revisited the question at much larger scale.

**What it is good for.** A compact example of a hypothesis stated before the data were
collected, tested against a proximity null, in a volume small enough to reason about.

**What it does not support.** Strong effect-size claims. The number of connected pairs with
known tuning on both sides is small, so treat the size of the effect as uncertain even though
its direction held.
