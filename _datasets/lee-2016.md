Lee and colleagues combined in vivo calcium imaging with EM reconstruction to test
whether functionally similar pyramidal neurons are preferentially connected. They are — a result
that MICrONS later revisited at scale.

**What it is good for.** A compact example of a hypothesis stated before the data were collected,
with a null model, in a volume small enough to reason about.

**What it does not support.** Strong effect-size claims: the number of connected pairs recovered
is small, and the confidence intervals are correspondingly wide.
