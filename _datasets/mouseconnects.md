MouseConnects, the Center for High-throughput Integrative Mouse Connectomics, is a
BRAIN CONNECTS project targeting 10 mm³ of mouse hippocampal formation at synaptic resolution.
That is roughly ten times the volume of H01 or MICrONS, and the projected data exceeds 10 PB.

**Why it matters to a learner now, before any data exists.** Everything on this site about
infrastructure, throughput and error budgets is scoped by projects of this size. The reason
Unit 04 spends its time on storage, alignment and versioning is that a volume like this cannot
be handled any other way.

**What it does not yet support.** Anything empirical. No data is released; the numbers above are
targets, not measurements. Treat this entry as a statement of intent and check the project page
for current status.
