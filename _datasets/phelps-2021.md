GridTape automates one of the most failure-prone steps in serial-section EM: collecting
sections onto a substrate that can go straight into a transmission EM. This dataset used it to
image the adult female *Drosophila* ventral nerve cord and reconstruct all 507 motor neurons
that control the legs and wings. The authors also released the GridTape instrument designs and
software.

**What it is good for.** The methods half of Unit 03. It shows how automating section
collection and TEM imaging together makes a large serial-section volume affordable for one
lab.

**What it does not support.** Whole-VNC connectivity. This reconstruction targets motor
neurons, not the full circuit. For the dense reconstruction of the same volume, see FANC.
