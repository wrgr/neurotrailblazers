GridTape automates the most failure-prone step in serial-section EM: collecting sections
onto a substrate without losing or folding them. This dataset used it to image the adult female
Drosophila ventral nerve cord and reconstruct 507 limb motor neurons.

**What it is good for.** The methods half of Unit 03: it is the clearest illustration of why
section collection, not imaging, is the throughput bottleneck.

**What it does not support.** Whole-VNC connectivity — this reconstruction targets motor neurons
rather than the full circuit.
