Most connectomics datasets are one species, one animal, one time point. This one is
built to compare, tracking cortical synapse development in mouse and primate.

**What it is good for.** The comparative question the field mostly cannot yet ask. Use it when
a learner assumes a mouse result transfers to human.

**What it does not support.** Whole-circuit analysis; the sampling is designed for synapse-level
statistics, not connectivity.
