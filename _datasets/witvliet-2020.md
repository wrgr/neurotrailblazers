Eight isogenic C. elegans individuals were reconstructed across postnatal stages. The
result is the field's best evidence on two questions that no single connectome can answer: what
changes with development, and what varies between genetically identical animals.

**What it is good for.** Calibrating how much of any single connectome is idiosyncratic. Anyone
about to make a claim from one volume should read this first.

**What it does not support.** Extrapolation to larger brains; the invariance of the worm lineage
is a special case.
