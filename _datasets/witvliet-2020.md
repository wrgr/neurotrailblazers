Eight genetically identical *C. elegans* were reconstructed at stages from birth to
adulthood. The result is the field's best evidence on two questions that no single connectome
can answer: what changes with development, and what varies between genetically identical
animals.

**What it is good for.** Calibrating how much of any single connectome is idiosyncratic. Read
it before you make a claim from one volume.

**What it does not support.** Extrapolation to larger brains. The worm's invariant cell lineage
is a special case.
