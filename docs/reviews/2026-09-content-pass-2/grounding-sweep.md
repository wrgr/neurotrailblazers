# Grounding sweep: technical training, reference library, datasets, tools, outreach

*Started 27 September 2026 on branch `compass-workshops`. Nothing committed.*

**Task.** Every item that the September 2026 audits left as "unsourced", "rule of thumb",
"typical value", "left unverified", "could not verify" or "needs a source" in this area is
listed below, then resolved one at a time. Each row is updated as soon as its item is
resolved, so progress survives an interruption.

**Area.** `technical-training/**` (units, atlas, proofreading tutorials, slide wrapper pages,
dictionary and `_data/connectomics_dictionary.yml`, `_data/technical_*.yml`),
`content-library/neuroanatomy/**`, `content-library/proofreading/**`,
`content-library/connectomics/**` (not `comparative-connectomics.md`),
`content-library/imaging/**` (not `beyond-em.md`), `_datasets/**`, `datasets/**`, `tools/**`,
`neuronauts/**`, `side-quests/**`, `initiatives/**`, `about.md`, `connectivity.md`,
`_data/connectivity.yml`, `course/decks/marp/0*.marp.md` and
`atlas-connectomics-reference.marp.md` (sources go in speaker notes; slide counts unchanged).

**Rule.** (a) Find the primary source and cite it inline in the page's own citation style,
recording the quoted sentence, table or figure here; or (b) if no source exists, convert the
claim to a labeled estimate with its basis on the page ("our arithmetic from ...", "this
site's rule of thumb"), or remove it if it carries no teaching value. No bare number may
read as measured.

**Sources of the list.** `docs/reviews/2026-09-site-audit/{technical-course,content-library-1,
content-library-2,datasets-tools,decks,modules,neuronauts-side-quests,front-door,README}.md`,
`docs/reviews/claim-audit/README.md` and the per-deck claim-audit files,
`docs/reviews/2026-09-fabrication-audit-{pages,modules}.md`, and
`docs/reviews/2026-09-site-audit/canonical-facts.md`. A grep of the area for
`typically|on the order of|roughly|about N|~N` near dataset and paper names adds rows in
section B.

**Status legend.** OPEN = not yet worked. SOURCED = primary source found and cited on the
page. RELABELED = no primary source; now an explicit estimate, rule of thumb or
attribution-as-given. REMOVED = deleted (no teaching value). OK = already sourced or
labeled correctly on inspection; no edit needed. UNRESOLVED = could not be settled; owner
decision listed at the end.
