        </p>
        <div style="background: #f8fafc; border-left: 3px solid #f59e0b; padding: 0.5rem 0.75rem; font-size: 0.8rem; color: #78350f; margin-bottom: 0.75rem;">

        </div>
        <a href="https://eyewire.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener" class="btn btn-secondary" style="font-size: 0.78rem; padding: 0.35rem 0.7rem;">Play EyeWire &rarr;</a>

      <div class="card" style="border: 1px solid #e2e8f0; border-radius: 10px; padding: 1.5rem; background: #fff; box-shadow: 0 2px 6px rgba(0,0,0,0.04);">

        </p>

          <strong>Major Scientific Milestone:</strong> Complete whole-brain wiring diagram published as a flagship collection in <a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07558-y" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Nature (2024)</a>.
        </div>
