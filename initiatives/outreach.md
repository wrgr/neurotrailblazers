  <!-- Section 1: Citizen Science & Crowdsourced Mapping -->
  <section class="section" style="margin-bottom: 3rem;">
    <div class="section-header">
      <h2 style="font-size: 1.6rem; color: #0f172a; border-bottom: 2px solid #e2e8f0; padding-bottom: 0.5rem; display: flex; align-items: center; gap: 0.5rem;">
        1. Citizen Science &amp; Crowdsourced Mapping
      </h2>
      <p style="color: #475569; font-size: 0.95rem;">Volunteers and students have traced neurons alongside professional labs. Two projects show what that can and cannot produce.</p>
    </div>

    <div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(320px, 1fr)); gap: 1.5rem; margin-top: 1.5rem;">
      
      <!-- EyeWire Card -->
      <div class="card" style="border: 1px solid #e2e8f0; border-radius: 10px; padding: 1.5rem; background: #fff; box-shadow: 0 2px 6px rgba(0,0,0,0.04);">
        <div style="display: flex; justify-content: space-between; align-items: flex-start; margin-bottom: 0.5rem;">
          <h3 style="margin: 0; font-size: 1.2rem; color: #1e293b;"><a href="https://eyewire.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener" style="color: #1a56db; text-decoration: none;">EyeWire &rarr;</a></h3>
          <span class="jc-tag" style="background: #fef3c7; color: #92400e; font-weight: 700;">120,000 players by 2014</span>
        </div>
        <p style="font-size: 0.88rem; color: #475569; line-height: 1.5; margin-bottom: 0.75rem;">
          Launched by Sebastian Seung's lab at MIT and later run from Princeton, EyeWire turned retinal neuron tracing into a web game. Players color in one neuron's branches, one small cube of EM data at a time.
        </p>
        <div style="background: #f8fafc; border-left: 3px solid #f59e0b; padding: 0.5rem 0.75rem; font-size: 0.8rem; color: #78350f; margin-bottom: 0.75rem;">
          <strong>Best-known result:</strong> Reconstructions made with EyeWire players showed that two bipolar cell types wire to different parts of starburst amacrine cell dendrites, evidence for a wiring model of direction selectivity (Kim et al., <a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/nature13240" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Nature 2014</a>). The analysis and the model were the lab's; the tracing was shared.
        </div>
        <a href="https://eyewire.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener" class="btn btn-secondary" style="font-size: 0.78rem; padding: 0.35rem 0.7rem;">Play EyeWire &rarr;</a>
      </div>

      <!-- FlyWire Consortium Card -->
      <div class="card" style="border: 1px solid #e2e8f0; border-radius: 10px; padding: 1.5rem; background: #fff; box-shadow: 0 2px 6px rgba(0,0,0,0.04);">
        <div style="display: flex; justify-content: space-between; align-items: flex-start; margin-bottom: 0.5rem;">
          <h3 style="margin: 0; font-size: 1.2rem; color: #1e293b;"><a href="https://codex.flywire.ai/" target="_blank" rel="noopener" style="color: #1a56db; text-decoration: none;">FlyWire Consortium &rarr;</a></h3>
          <span class="jc-tag" style="background: #dbeafe; color: #1e40af; font-weight: 700;">Whole Adult Brain</span>
        </div>
        <p style="font-size: 0.88rem; color: #475569; line-height: 1.5; margin-bottom: 0.75rem;">
          According to the consortium's press release, 287 researchers in at least 76 labs, plus volunteers, took part; the paper reports 33 person-years of proofreading for the first complete connectome of an adult fruit fly brain: 139,255 neurons and about 54.5 million chemical synapses.
        </p>
        <div style="background: #f8fafc; border-left: 3px solid #3b82f6; padding: 0.5rem 0.75rem; font-size: 0.8rem; color: #1e3a8a; margin-bottom: 0.75rem;">
          <strong>Key paper:</strong> The whole-brain wiring diagram, published with companion papers in <a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07558-y" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Nature (2024)</a>.
        </div>
        <a href="https://codex.flywire.ai/" target="_blank" rel="noopener" class="btn btn-secondary" style="font-size: 0.78rem; padding: 0.35rem 0.7rem;">Explore FlyWire Codex &rarr;</a>
      </div>

      <!-- Allen / BigNeuron card -->
      <div class="card" style="border: 1px solid #e2e8f0; border-radius: 10px; padding: 1.5rem; background: #fff; box-shadow: 0 2px 6px rgba(0,0,0,0.04);">
        <div style="display: flex; justify-content: space-between; align-items: flex-start; margin-bottom: 0.5rem;">
          <h3 style="margin: 0; font-size: 1.2rem; color: #1e293b;"><a href="https://portal.brain-map.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener" style="color: #1a56db; text-decoration: none;">Allen Brain Map &amp; BigNeuron &rarr;</a></h3>
          <span class="jc-tag" style="background: #ede9fe; color: #6d28d9; font-weight: 700;">Community Standards</span>
        </div>
        <p style="font-size: 0.88rem; color: #475569; line-height: 1.5; margin-bottom: 0.75rem;">
          Open brain atlases from the Allen Institute for Brain Science. BigNeuron brought computational labs together to benchmark automated neuron tracing in light microscopy. It is a community benchmark rather than citizen science, and is listed here because it set shared standards.
        </p>
        <div style="background: #f8fafc; border-left: 3px solid #8b5cf6; padding: 0.5rem 0.75rem; font-size: 0.8rem; color: #4c1d95; margin-bottom: 0.75rem;">
          <strong>Key paper:</strong> BigNeuron benchmark and performance prediction (Manubens-Gil et al., <a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-023-01848-5" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Nature Methods 2023</a>).
        </div>
        <a href="https://portal.brain-map.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener" class="btn btn-secondary" style="font-size: 0.78rem; padding: 0.35rem 0.7rem;">Visit Allen Brain Map &rarr;</a>
      </div>

    </div>
  </section>

        3. Open Data Ecosystems &amp; Community Portals
      </h2>
      <p style="color: #475569; font-size: 0.95rem;">Where the public volumes live, and how you query them.</p>
    </div>

    <div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(280px, 1fr)); gap: 1.25rem; margin-top: 1.5rem;">
      
      <div class="card" style="border: 1px solid #cbd5e1; border-radius: 8px; padding: 1.25rem; background: #fff;">
        <h4 style="margin: 0 0 0.35rem 0; font-size: 1rem;"><a href="https://bossdb.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener" style="color: #1a56db; text-decoration: none;">bossDB (JHU APL) &rarr;</a></h4>
        <p style="font-size: 0.82rem; color: #475569; line-height: 1.45; margin: 0;">
          The Brain Observatory Storage Service and Database. A cloud database for large EM, X-ray and light microscopy volumes, and a BRAIN Initiative data archive.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="card" style="border: 1px solid #cbd5e1; border-radius: 8px; padding: 1.25rem; background: #fff;">
        <h4 style="margin: 0 0 0.35rem 0; font-size: 1rem;"><a href="https://neuprint.janelia.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener" style="color: #1a56db; text-decoration: none;">neuPrint (Janelia) &rarr;</a></h4>
        <p style="font-size: 0.82rem; color: #475569; line-height: 1.45; margin: 0;">
          Graph database portal allowing researchers to search neurons by cell type, find direct upstream/downstream synaptic partners, and analyze connectivity matrices with Cypher queries.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="card" style="border: 1px solid #cbd5e1; border-radius: 8px; padding: 1.25rem; background: #fff;">
        <h4 style="margin: 0 0 0.35rem 0; font-size: 1rem;"><a href="https://www.nwb.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener" style="color: #1a56db; text-decoration: none;">Neurodata Without Borders (NWB) &rarr;</a></h4>
        <p style="font-size: 0.82rem; color: #475569; line-height: 1.45; margin: 0;">
          The community data standard for neurophysiology recordings. It stores the functional side of a structure-function study; it does not store or align EM volumes.
        </p>
      </div>
