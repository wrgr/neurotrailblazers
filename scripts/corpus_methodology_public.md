## How this paper list was built

- We started from a small, hand-picked set of milestone papers spanning the
  field's history, organisms, and major techniques.
- We found more papers by searching for those milestones' own topics, then
  following citation links one hop outward — requiring a paper to be cited by
  *several* independent papers already in the collection before adding it, so
  the list doesn't drown in generic background literature.
- A small number of very recent (2025–2026) papers are marked **provisional**:
  too new to have accumulated many citations yet, but with at least one real,
  verified link to a paper already in the collection. These will graduate to
  full status as more evidence accumulates.
- Every paper's inclusion traces back to a real search or citation link —
  nothing was carried over unverified from earlier, undocumented collections.
- This corpus focuses on nanoscale/electron-microscopy connectomics. Related
  fields — clinical/fMRI connectomics, broader neuroscience — are
  intentionally underrepresented, by design, not by oversight.
