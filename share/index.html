Review the spoken pitch

Read it aloud. Aim for 65–130 words, roughly 30–60 seconds at 130 words per minute. Read it as a conversation with a student. End by inviting them to ask a question, and keep the study scope and uncertainty.

Editorial prompt for an optional AI-assisted rewrite

Make the poster
Lead with a memorable question and a one-sentence overview. Use the panels to explain the science, what the team found and where the evidence stops. Each panel has its own space and word limit.
