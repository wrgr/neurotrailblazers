## Worked calculations

There are **12 synapses**, **3/4 connected pairs (75%)**, **12/4 = 3 synapses per
eligible pair**, and **12/3 = 4 synapses per connected pair**. The conditional mean
excludes the zero pair. Neither mean is a firing rate or physiological weight.

## Claim sorting

1. **A.** Six reviewed synapses in the specified region is a structural observation,
   under the exercise's assumption that the counts and identities are correct.
2. **B.** The inference requires a relationship between count and physiological
   strength, with other determinants controlled. A defensible replacement is “A1
   makes more observed synapses onto B1 than B2.” Testing physiological strength
   needs an appropriate functional measurement.
3. **C.** No activity or causal evidence was supplied. “A1 has an anatomical route
   to B1” is supported. Task-linked recording and a suitable perturbation would be
   needed to test the causal claim.
4. **Unsupported extrapolation, C in this exercise's scheme.** The observed zero is
   region-limited. Replace it with “No A2-to-B1 synapse was found in the reviewed
   region.” Establishing absence outside it needs additional structural coverage
   and stated detection sensitivity. This is a sampling problem, not necessarily a
   need for physiology. Accept that distinction rather than grading the letter alone.

## Example study brief

**Question:** Within a prespecified reconstructed region, is the observed fraction
of A-to-B pairs containing at least one synapse greater than for A-to-C pairs with
comparable opportunities for contact?

**Measurement:** Acquire synapse-resolving EM and reconstruct the relevant neurites
and partner identities. The analysis unit is a directed cell pair within the region.
For an illustrative pilot, plan 20 eligible A-to-B and 20 matched A-to-C pairs. This
is a proposed pilot size, not a power calculation or evidence of population coverage.

**Endpoint:** Difference in connected-pair fractions, in percentage points. Report
both numerators and eligible-pair denominators. Define eligibility before inspecting
the outcomes, including boundary exclusions and reconstruction coverage.

**Null:** No difference in connectivity associated with target label after matching
on contact opportunity and location. Permute B/C labels only within prespecified
matched sets while holding the observed graph fixed. State that label exchangeability
within those sets is an assumption. Pairs sharing neurons are not independent replicates.

**Error check:** Review uncertain contacts and identities without knowing which
outcome favors the hypothesis. Repeat the endpoint with uncertain pairs included
and excluded. A difference that reverses under plausible corrections weakens the claim.

**Non-claim:** This pilot would not establish stronger physiological transmission
or behavioral causation. Those require additional functional evidence. A single
region also does not establish a general rule across brains.

## Feedback guide

Score four dimensions **0–2 each**: correct denominators; evidence-matched claims;
an explicit population/comparison; and an error check plus non-claim. Use 2 for an
explicit, coherent response, 1 for a recoverable omission, and 0 for absent or
contradictory reasoning. A proficient response has at least 6/8 and no zero in
evidence-matched claims or non-claim/error reasoning. This is a local teaching rubric,
not a validated assessment instrument.

Do not reward an elaborate statistical method without a defensible denominator.
Ask “What observation would change your mind?” before suggesting a method.
