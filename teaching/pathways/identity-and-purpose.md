*Workshop 9 of the [Professional Pathways workshops]({{ '/teaching/pathways/' | relative_url }}).
90 minutes, no slides required.*

- [Learner worksheet]({{ '/teaching/pathways/identity-and-purpose-activity/' | relative_url }})
- [Instructor model responses]({{ '/teaching/pathways/identity-and-purpose-answers/' | relative_url }})
- Background: [Belonging]({{ '/hidden-curriculum/belonging/' | relative_url }}), especially
  *The test* and *When the answer is that the environment is bad*

**Question:** Do I belong here, and what would it mean if the answer is complicated?

## Outcomes and preparation

Learners apply the belonging test to a moment: can the missing thing be named? They
sort an invented month into knowledge gaps and environment signals; plan three
checkable contributions with dates; and write a private diagnosis of one moment of their
own. The contribution plan is the shareable artifact. The diagnosis is never collected.

[Module 01]({{ '/modules/module01/' | relative_url }}) has learners write a motivation
statement: why connectomics, and how they will keep going through tedious stretches.
This workshop does not ask why. It asks about standing: whether a feeling of not
belonging is a gap that closes with a named piece of knowledge, or a signal about the
environment that effort will not fix. The hub recommends running it in the first month,
not after someone is already deciding whether to leave.

Before class, read the belonging page in full, including *What this page does not
cover*. Write on a card the names and contact routes for your institution's counseling
service, ombuds office and one named program contact. You will read them aloud. **The
case is invented.** Nobody is asked to say whether they feel they belong.

## Timed plan and facilitator cues

- **0–10 min:** set the frame first. Say: "This is not counseling, and nobody will be
  asked to share anything about themselves. Here is who to talk to if something today
  lands hard." Read the card. If this session follows Professional Conduct, ask who
  found out what a rung-3 office must report; keep it procedural. Then name one specific thing you once did not know that
  everyone around you seemed to know, and what happened to the feeling once you learned
  it. Do not ask for a show of hands.
- **10–20 min:** write the two sentences from the belonging page on the board: "I do not
  know things everyone here seems to know" and "I do not belong in this room." Read the
  three branches of the test aloud. Stress the third: you named it, you closed it, and
  the feeling did not move.
- **20–45 min:** pairs complete worksheet sections 1 and 2 on the invented case. At
  minute 32, check where pairs have put moment 5, where Inés's proofreading is credited
  to someone else twice. Expect some pairs to explain it away as a misunderstanding,
  and a few to treat every moment as an environment signal.
- **45–55 min:** debrief. Take moment 5 first. Ask: "What would a contribution plan do
  about this one?" The answer is: not much. Contributions build standing; they do not
  fix an environment. Then ask one pair for Dev's gap.
- **55–75 min:** individuals complete section 3: a private diagnosis and their own
  contribution plan. Circulate, but do not read over shoulders.
- **75–85 min:** pairs swap *only the contribution plan*. The partner checks one thing:
  could someone else verify each contribution happened?
- **85–90 min:** exit ticket. Repeat the support card. Name the follow-through task and
  the date you will ask about it.

## Facilitator cautions

Do not tell anyone they belong. The belonging page says why: it cannot be installed by
someone else. Specificity helps; reassurance does not.

Do not turn environment problems into individual resilience problems. If a learner
recognizes several environment signals in their own setting, the right response is the
page's: document, find one outside person, and treat leaving as a legitimate option. If
what surfaces sounds like harassment or discrimination, check your reporting obligations
before asking anything more, and point to the [Conflict]({{ '/hidden-curriculum/conflict/' | relative_url }})
page and the named offices.

If a learner is distressed, or describes something that sounds like persistent anxiety
or depression, do not work on it in the room. Pause the activity for them, speak
privately, and refer them to the counseling service. The belonging page says plainly
that clinical conditions are outside its scope; so is this workshop.

Do not single out learners you believe are first-generation or career-changers. The case
has one of each so that nobody has to say which they are.

## Assessment and follow-through

Use the four-dimension rubric on the model responses page. Collect sections 1 and 2,
and the contribution plan from section 3 if the learner chooses. The private diagnosis
is never collected.

**Follow-through:** make the first contribution on your plan this week. If your diagnosis
named a gap, close it and note whether the feeling moved. Start [Future
Forward]({{ '/teaching/pathways/future-forward/' | relative_url }}) by asking who made
their first contribution and what happened next.
