This map runs a **two-day workshop of eight 90-minute slots**, four a day. It uses the
[four-session block]({{ '/teaching/sequence/' | relative_url }}), the optional Ethics and
Governance session, Professional Pathways workshop 1 (Orientation), the
[MICrONS real-data lab]({{ '/notebooks/microns-lab/' | relative_url }}) and a closing
revision studio. Each learner carries one study brief from the first session to a
revised claim at the end of Day 2. See the
[syllabus maps overview]({{ '/teaching/syllabi/' | relative_url }}) for audience,
prerequisites and assessment assumptions. It is a draft built from the materials and
has not been run as a whole.

**Time budget.** Each day is 360 minutes of teaching plus three 15-minute breaks:
**6 hours 45 minutes, excluding lunch**. This is the block's own arithmetic for all
four sessions in one day. Two days give 12 contact hours. Advance work is 45 timed
minutes before Day 1 (lab norms, 25; the Synapse Detection reading, 20), plus reading
the Orientation worksheet case and installing the MICrONS lab requirements. The install
has no stated time on the lab page; do it before Day 1 so failures surface early. The
evening between the days carries the 25-minute Ethics reading and finishing the
methods record. Without Ethics, Day 2 is three slots and two breaks: **5 hours
0 minutes**. Do not stretch the other slots to fill the gap.

## Day 1

### Slot 1: What is expected here, and how do you find out?

**Pathways workshop 1, Orientation** (MERIT: selection and orientation).
[Plan]({{ '/teaching/pathways/orientation/' | relative_url }}) ·
[Worksheet]({{ '/teaching/pathways/orientation-activity/' | relative_url }}) ·
[Model responses]({{ '/teaching/pathways/orientation-answers/' | relative_url }})

It opens the workshop because it sets how the room will work: questions with a
deliverable and a date, and "uncertain" as a legitimate answer. The plan's follow-through
is a conversation with a supervisor about four weeks later. In a two-day format that
happens after the workshop, so name who will prompt it.

**Before.** [Lab norms]({{ '/hidden-curriculum/lab-norms/' | relative_url }}) (25 minutes)
and the worksheet case. **Artifacts.** Worksheet sections 1 and 2; section 3, the
personal expectations list, stays with the learner. **Feedback.** The Orientation
[feedback rubric]({{ '/teaching/pathways/orientation-answers/' | relative_url }}#feedback-rubric).

### Slot 2: What can a wiring diagram establish?

**Session 1, Introduction to Connectomics.**
[Timed plan and slides]({{ '/teaching/lectures/connectomics-01-introduction/' | relative_url }}#teach-a-90-minute-session) ·
[Worksheet]({{ '/teaching/lectures/introduction-activity/' | relative_url }}) ·
[Model responses]({{ '/teaching/lectures/introduction-answers/' | relative_url }})

**Before.** Nothing beyond the advance work. **Artifact.** Study brief v1: a structural
endpoint with a denominator, a comparison and an explicit non-claim. **Feedback.** The
Introduction key's [four-dimension rubric]({{ '/teaching/lectures/introduction-answers/' | relative_url }}#feedback-guide).
Return the scored brief before slot 3; learners revise it there.

### Slot 3: What errors could change that result?

**Session 2, Synapse Detection.**
[Timed plan and slides]({{ '/teaching/lectures/synapse-detection/' | relative_url }}) ·
[Worksheet]({{ '/teaching/lectures/synapse-detection-activity/' | relative_url }}) ·
[Model responses]({{ '/teaching/lectures/synapse-detection-answers/' | relative_url }})

**Before.** The block's between-session reading becomes advance work, as the
[sequence page]({{ '/teaching/sequence/' | relative_url }}#preparation-and-pacing) says for
a single-day event: the opening section and sections 1, 2 and 7 of
[Synapse Detection]({{ '/content-library/infrastructure/synapse-detection/' | relative_url }})
(20 minutes). **Artifacts.** An audit plan and a revised brief that names its most
damaging error. **Feedback.** Synapse Detection
[rubric]({{ '/teaching/lectures/synapse-detection-answers/' | relative_url }}#feedback-guide).

### Slot 4: Can another person reproduce the number in your figure?

**Session 3, Tools and Methods.**
[Timed plan and slides]({{ '/teaching/lectures/connectomics-02-tools-and-methods/' | relative_url }}#teach-a-90-minute-session) ·
[Worksheet and offline query]({{ '/teaching/lectures/tools-and-methods-activity/' | relative_url }}) ·
[Model responses]({{ '/teaching/lectures/tools-and-methods-answers/' | relative_url }})

**Before.** Bring the revised brief and audit. **Artifacts.** A recoverable methods
record and a raw-storage estimate. **Feedback.** Tools and Methods
[rubric]({{ '/teaching/lectures/tools-and-methods-answers/' | relative_url }}#feedback-guide):
no zero in provenance or interpretation.

**Evening (about 45 minutes).** Finish the methods record (20 minutes, the 10-week map's
allowance) and read [Ethics and Governance]({{ '/content-library/connectomics/ethics-and-governance/' | relative_url }})
(25 minutes). Anyone whose lab install failed should say so before Day 2 starts.

## Day 2

### Slot 1: Does the structural result exceed what the null explains?

**Session 4, Algorithms and Applications.**
[Timed plan and slides]({{ '/teaching/lectures/connectomics-03-algorithms-and-applications/' | relative_url }}#teach-a-90-minute-session) ·
[Worksheet and offline analysis]({{ '/teaching/lectures/algorithms-and-applications-activity/' | relative_url }}) ·
[Model responses]({{ '/teaching/lectures/algorithms-and-applications-answers/' | relative_url }})

**Before.** Bring the brief, audit and methods record. **Artifact.** An analysis card.
**Feedback.** Algorithms and Applications
[rubric]({{ '/teaching/lectures/algorithms-and-applications-answers/' | relative_url }}#feedback-guide).

### Slot 2: Does the reasoning hold on real, versioned data?

**MICrONS real-data lab.** The [lab]({{ '/notebooks/microns-lab/' | relative_url }}) is the
real-data version of Sessions 3 and 4, so it follows them directly. It reads static public
exports pinned to materialization v1507, so no account is needed. Learners need a laptop
with Python 3.11–3.13, about 90 MB of downloads and 2 GB of free memory. Run it in the
four steps the [10-week map]({{ '/teaching/syllabi/10-week/' | relative_url }}#week-4-what-does-the-image-support-and-what-does-a-reproducible-query-cost)
gives: install check and pairing (15 minutes), run and compare with the archived outputs
(35), methods record and the v1412 → v1507 drift section (25), and a debrief on which
null the headline should use (15).

**Offline fallback.** Read the archived executed notebook on the lab page, or run Unit 04
lab Part B (the capacity plan, no data) plus the Session 3 offline query under a second
inclusion threshold.

**Swap for one module kit.** For a cohort heading into proofreading work, the
[Module 07 kit]({{ '/teaching/sessions/module07/' | relative_url }}) can take this slot
instead. Its run of show is 60 minutes; use the remaining 30 for the release memo. It
triages a synthetic 45-flag error report and runs a metrics script (Python 3, no
packages), and it has [model responses]({{ '/teaching/answers/module07/' | relative_url }}).
Grade the release memo against the kit's assessment criteria in place of the lab's
methods record. Do not run both: there is no slot for the second.

**Artifacts.** A methods record with the v1507 version, inclusion rules and file hashes,
and a note on what the drift would do to a stale root-ID join. **Feedback.** The
Unit 04 [rubric]({{ '/technical-training/04-volume-reconstruction-infrastructure/' | relative_url }}#rubric)
rows for storage, compute and labor, with its Version discipline row applied to the
inclusion rules.

### Slot 3 (optional): Who may use the data, and who gets credit?

**Ethics and Governance.**
[Timed plan and slides]({{ '/teaching/lectures/ethics-and-governance/' | relative_url }}) ·
[Worksheet]({{ '/teaching/lectures/ethics-and-governance-activity/' | relative_url }}) ·
[Model responses]({{ '/teaching/lectures/ethics-and-governance-answers/' | relative_url }})

**Before.** [Ethics and Governance]({{ '/content-library/connectomics/ethics-and-governance/' | relative_url }})
(25 minutes, the evening before). **Artifacts.** A license audit table and a governance
note; the brief gains a license, an ethics-statement location and a credit rule.
**Feedback.** Ethics [rubric]({{ '/teaching/lectures/ethics-and-governance-answers/' | relative_url }}#feedback-guide):
no zero in license accuracy or handling of uncertainty. The session is not legal advice.

### Slot 4: What claim does the whole package support?

**Revision studio and share-out.** This slot has no packaged plan; it is built from the
rubrics already used. Suggested run:

1. Lay out the package: brief, audit, methods record, analysis card, lab methods record
   and, if run, governance note (15 minutes).
2. Rewrite the final claim so every qualifier is earned by an artifact on the table, and
   state one non-claim (20 minutes).
3. Pairs score each other's final claim on the Introduction key's
   [four-dimension rubric]({{ '/teaching/lectures/introduction-answers/' | relative_url }}#feedback-guide),
   then revise (30 minutes).
4. Three-minute share-outs from volunteers: the claim, the artifact that most changed it,
   and the next check each would run (25 minutes).

**Artifacts.** A revised final claim and a one-page study package. **Feedback.** The
four-dimension rubric, applied to the revision rather than the first draft.

## What this map uses and omits

**Uses.** All four sessions of the [block]({{ '/teaching/sequence/' | relative_url }}),
plus Ethics and Governance. Pathways workshop 1, Orientation. The MICrONS real-data lab,
or the Module 07 kit in its place. One unstructured studio slot that reuses the
Introduction rubric.

**Omitted.** Every technical-unit lab except the Unit 04 fallback, all nine units as
reading, the other 24 module kits and the other nine Pathways workshops. The 10-week
and 16-week maps show where they fit. For extra practice between or after the days, the
[unit item pool]({{ '/teaching/assessment/units/' | relative_url }}) and the
[lecture item bank]({{ '/teaching/assessment/' | relative_url }}) have public formative
items with worked answers.

## What a two-day format cannot do

- **Build judgment on images.** Nothing here has learners call synapses, compartments or
  glia in EM. The Unit 05–07 studios and calibration labs need patch sets and repetition
  over weeks. Two days can explain why calibration matters; they cannot calibrate anyone.
- **Space the practice.** The four-session block was written with a reading between
  Sessions 1 and 2, and the units expect reading before labs. Compressing it into two
  days moves that reading into advance work and one evening. Retention after a
  compressed block is not measured here.
- **Run the Pathways follow-through.** Orientation's supervisor conversation happens
  weeks later. Without a second contact, nobody checks it.
- **Proofread.** No slot gives learners a segmentation to edit. The Module 07 swap
  triages flags on paper; it does not train an eye.
- **Assess summatively.** Every key used here is public. Treat all scores as formative
  feedback.

## Suggested feedback

This is a suggestion, not a validated scheme. For a certificate of completion rather
than a grade, use the first row as the criterion and the rest as feedback only.

| Component | What to look for | Rubric |
|---|---|---|
| Study package | Brief, audit, methods record and analysis card all present, each revised once | Session keys' feedback guides |
| Lab methods record | Version, inclusion rules and hashes stated; drift note names what a stale join loses | Unit 04 rubric, Version discipline row |
| Governance note (if Ethics ran) | License recorded accurately; the conflict recorded rather than resolved | Ethics feedback guide |
| Final claim | Every qualifier earned by an artifact; one non-claim stated | Introduction four-dimension rubric |
| Orientation | Sections 1 and 2 handed in; section 3 never collected | Orientation feedback rubric |
