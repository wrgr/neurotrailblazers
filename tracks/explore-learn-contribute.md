# Explore → Learn → Contribute

NeuroTrailblazers should let people enter at different depths without creating separate content silos.

## EXPLORE

**For:** public audiences, new learners, younger students, families, educators.

Suggested route:

**01 → 04 → 05 → 11 → 12 → Module 22 companion**

Use:
- public interactive;
- Atlas vs. Connectome;
- From Projectome to Synapse;
- 1 mm³ scale demo;
- Impact Wall;
- Brain Map Passport.

### Exit evidence

> I can explain what different brain maps tell us, why CONNECTS uses multiple technologies, and what remains unknown.

---

## LEARN

**For:** high school, undergraduate, early graduate learners.

Use the 25-module curriculum with authentic data/resources embedded.

Examples:
- Allen ABC Atlas;
- MICrONS;
- FlyWire/Codex;
- MouseConnects workflow;
- IC3/APEX/CONNECTS tools and data as released.

### Exit evidence

> I can produce a reproducible technical artifact and defend the scientific claim it supports.

---

## CONTRIBUTE

**For:** advanced undergraduate, graduate, research trainees.

Recommended concentration:

**03, 06–15, 18–21, 23–25**

Authentic tasks may include:
- proofreading/QC;
- morphology/skeletonization;
- query notebooks;
- pipeline validation;
- graph analysis;
- reproducibility audits;
- scientific communication;
- public-engagement artifacts;
- research software/data documentation.

### Exit evidence

> I can produce work with enough provenance, quality evidence, and documentation that another research team can inspect it and potentially build on it.

## The handoff principle

The deeper the learner goes, the more NeuroTrailblazers should hand them to **real center/project infrastructure** rather than keep them inside educational replicas.
